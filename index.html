Problem

The pet industry still lacks a trusted data layer.

Cross-border travel, breeding transparency, and service reputation all depend on records that are easy to lose, hard to verify, and simple to manipulate.

Fragmented medical and quarantine records

Paper documents and isolated databases make international mobility slow, stressful, and vulnerable to forgery.

Breeding and lineage opacity

Without verifiable provenance, fraud in breeding, health claims, and ownership history remains common.

Reputation manipulation

Review fraud and fake social proof undermine trust in pet sitters, dog walkers, and service providers.

Solution

A premium identity and trust stack for pets.

Pet ID as an SBT

Each pet receives a non-transferable identity anchor that accumulates lifecycle data across care events.

Architecture

Built to fit into veterinary workflows, not fight them.

The design lets clinics issue proofs from their existing EMR software without requiring staff to think about wallets or gas fees.

01Vet-DID login verifies the clinician and clinic identity.
02EMR extracts only the public fields needed for quarantine and proof issuance.
03ERC-4337 handles gas abstraction through the relayer layer.
04The signed certificate is attached to the pet’s SBT instantly.
Tokenomics

Utility-first economics with real sinks.

The model is designed around service usage, staking discipline, and continuous demand from B2B partners.

Sources

  • M2E activity rewards.
  • Verified reputation participation rewards.
  • Data contribution incentives for clinics and users.
  • Partner campaigns and service-based emissions.

Sinks

  • Marketplace and escrow fee burns.
  • B2B buyback demand for ads and analytics.
  • Slashing of misbehavior collateral.
  • Lock-up incentives for premium utility usage.
Position the token as a pure utility and reward instrument. Avoid language that implies dividend, revenue share, or investment return.
